Nutrition Copilot Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 4, 2026

Nutrition Copilot is a personal nutrition tracking app. It may connect to WHOOP to read wellness data such as recovery, sleep, cycles, workouts, strain, and profile information.

Data We Access

If you connect WHOOP, the app may access WHOOP data you explicitly authorize, including recovery, sleep, cycle, workout, and profile data.

How Data Is Used

This data is used only to personalize nutrition guidance, such as helping estimate how meals fit into your day based on activity, recovery, and sleep context.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your data. We do not share your WHOOP data with third parties except as needed to operate the app.

Data Storage

Data may be stored locally or in backend services used by the app. You can revoke WHOOP access from your WHOOP account at any time.
